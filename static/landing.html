EMU Regulation Assistant

A local demo that answers staff questions about official EMU rules and regulations using cited evidence from mevzuat.emu.edu.tr and linked official PDFs.

  • English and Turkish questions are routed to separate regulation corpora.
  • Answers include citations and refuse when evidence is insufficient.
  • Informational only — not an official final university decision.
Open regulation assistant

For diagnostics, metrics, and retrieval debugging, open the assistant and switch to the Diagnostics tab. If your deployment uses an admin token, enter it in the diagnostics authentication field; it is stored only for the current tab and sent in an authorization header.
